## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance visual language tailored for next-generation mobile energy ecosystems, portable power hardware, and energy subscription platforms. The aesthetic merges precision engineering with kinetic energy, projecting reliability, high autonomy, and refined modern technology.

The audience consists of digital nomads, creators, remote professionals, and outdoor enthusiasts who demand absolute dependability and industrial design sophistication. The experience evokes a sense of limitless power and clarity: calm, deep charcoal environments pierced by sharp electric luminescence.

The style fuses High-Contrast Dark Minimalism with Luminescent Tactile Accents:
- Deep obsidian foundations anchor the interface, minimizing visual fatigue and power consumption.
- Electric orange serves as an intentional kinetic catalyst, reserved strictly for primary actions, active battery states, charging metrics, and interactive focal points.
- Smooth contours, subtle translucent backdrops, and soft ambient glows convey industrial finesse and hardware-software harmony.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around dark-mode supremacy, establishing an immersive, battery-efficient canvas with surgical pops of dynamic energy.

- **Primary (`#FF5722`)**: Pure kinetic energy. Applied to critical conversion triggers, active power flows, high-priority status badges, and radiant focal points.
- **Secondary (`#FF9100`)**: Solar charge and secondary kinetic flow. Pairs with the primary in high-contrast energy gradients and visual telemetry graphs.
- **Tertiary (`#00E5FF`)**: High-voltage safety and AC/DC output indicators, reserve diagnostics, and cool state feedback.
- **Neutral Foundation (`#0F1115`)**: Obsidian background matrix.
  - Surface 0 (Base Canvas): `#0F1115`
  - Surface 1 (Cards, elevated containers): `#16181E`
  - Surface 2 (Overlays, secondary inputs, inactive buttons): `#20232B`
  - Surface 3 (Borders, structural dividers): `#2A2E39`
  - Text Primary: `#FFFFFF` (100% white contrast against deep charcoal)
  - Text Secondary: `#9EACB9` (balanced legibility for telemetry details and labels)
  - Text Muted: `#5C6470` (structural captions and metadata)

## Typography

Typography establishes an industrial yet digital aesthetic, combining geometric technological discipline with effortless editorial legibility.

- **Headlines & Metric Data (Space Grotesk)**: Chosen for its subtle technical nuance and mechanical precision. Display styles convey wattage, voltage, battery longevity metrics, and brand statements with absolute visual impact.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Offers soft geometric shapes, open counters, and high clarity across dark canvases, preventing reading fatigue in subscription management dashboards and technical manuals.
- **Labels, Telemetry & Tags (Space Grotesk)**: Monospaced-leaning characteristics in uppercase configurations elevate industrial readout authenticity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model emphasizes airy, organized composition on dark canvases to give hardware renderings and real-time charging telemetry room to breathe.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column responsive grid system, 2.5rem outer canvas margin, 1.5rem gutters. Centered containers cap at 1280px for desktop dashboards and 1440px for cinematic marketing pages.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column layout, 2rem outer margins, 1.25rem gutters. Flexible card grids reflow from 3–4 columns down to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 1.25rem outer margins and 1rem gutters. Metric readouts stack vertically or scroll horizontally via snap carousels.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm**: Multiples of 8px dictate component separation, using `space-xl` (40px) between card sections and `space-md` (16px) for interior groupings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh, generic drop shadows in favor of luminescence, tonal surface shifts, and atmospheric light pollution.

1. **Surface Tiering**:
   - Level 0 (`#0F1115`): Global screen canvas.
   - Level 1 (`#16181E`): Base card container layer with a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`).
   - Level 2 (`#20232B`): Raised controls, floating modals, or hover-lifted cards.
2. **Kinetic Glow (Energy Elevation)**:
   - Primary Interactive Glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -2px rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.45), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.25)`.
   - Subtle Active Edge: `0 0 12px 0 rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.3)`.
3. **Glassmorphic Overlays**:
   - Navigation bars, sticky dock controllers, and HUD overlays utilize `background: rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.75)`, coupled with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle top-lit border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The shape system mirrors the industrial geometry of modern power stations: ergonomically molded corners, tactile recessed grips, and high-precision finishes.

- Standard UI containers (Cards, panels, modal windows) utilize `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem) to reinforce smooth, consumer-hardware touchpoints.
- Intermediate elements (Inputs, nested sub-cards, segmented controls) utilize `rounded-lg` (0.75rem to 1rem).
- Micro components (Buttons, pill badges, status indicators) employ fluid pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`) to emphasize touchability and fluid mobility.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Energy Button**: Gradient background `linear-gradient(135deg, #FF5722 0%, #FF9100 100%)`, pure white text, Space Grotesk bold label, rounded pill shape. Default state includes a soft orange ambient glow. On hover, the glow expands (`box-shadow: 0 0 28px rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.55)`), accompanied by a subtle 1px upward scale transition.
- **Secondary (Ghost) Button**: Surface `#16181E` fill with a `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` border. On hover, border shifts to `#FF5722` with muted orange text glow.
- **Tertiary Minimal Button**: Transparent fill, white or muted gray text, underline glow transition on hover.

### Cards
- Constructed with `#16181E` surface, rounded `1.5rem` (`rounded-2xl`) corners, and a perimeter hairline stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- Cards housing live power telemetry or active subscriptions feature a localized kinetic gradient sweep or a top-edge highlight (`1px solid rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.6)`).

### Input Fields
- Dark recessed background (`#0F1115`), `rounded-lg` (0.75rem), `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Focus State: Border transitions to `#FF5722` with a diffuse `0 0 0 3px rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.18)` ring. Typography remains crisp white with floating labels rendered in Space Grotesk.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped status chips (`rounded-full`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`).
- **Live / Charging**: Background `rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.12)`, text `#FF5722`, accompanied by a pulsing 6px circular green/orange LED dot.
- **Standard / Idle**: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, text `#9EACB9`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Radio controls are dual-ring circuits: exterior dark ring `#2A2E39`, animating to `#FF5722` perimeter with an inner radiant orange node when active.
- Checkboxes maintain smooth `0.375rem` corners with checkmark paths rendering in `#0F1115` against a saturated `#FF5722` active fill.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Telemetry Gauge / Energy HUD**: Radial or segmented horizontal track with `#20232B` inactive slots and `#FF5722` glowing live energy segments showing remaining kWh and discharge velocity.
- **Subscription Swap Matrix**: Dual-panel cards visualizing station reserve, battery lifecycle degradation, and one-tap exchange hub locations.
